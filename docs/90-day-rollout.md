# 90-day rollout

Twelve weeks, twelve milestones. Sequenced so each step proves itself before the next is built on top of it.

> **The rule.** If a week's milestone is not working, do not start the next week's. Climb one rung at a time.

---

## Week 1, memory bootstrap

**Goal:** complete `prompts/08-memory-bootstrap.txt`.

- Day 1: read the bracketed placeholders. Decide what is private versus what you are willing to commit to memory.
- Day 2: fill in USER, COMPANY, FEEDBACK blocks. Three sentences per block is enough.
- Day 3: fill in PROJECT and REFERENCE blocks.
- Day 4: paste the filled prompt into a fresh Claude conversation. Save the file as `my-profile.md` in project knowledge.
- Day 5: ask Claude "what do you know about how I work?" If the answer feels uncannily accurate, you are done. If not, the bootstrap is too thin. Add five more sentences.

**Done when:** Claude can describe how you work to you and you do not cringe.

---

## Week 2, setup interview

**Goal:** run `prompts/09-setup-interview.txt` and capture the output.

- Day 1: paste the interview prompt into the same conversation.
- Day 2 to 4: answer the five rounds, one per day. Each round takes ten to fifteen minutes.
- Day 5: Claude proposes the personal roadmap. Save it as `my-roadmap.md` in project knowledge.

**Done when:** you have a written, role-specific roadmap that names the first three skills to install.

---

## Week 3, the Ladder and the Power-Words

**Goal:** install `skills/11-superintelligence-ladder` and `skills/12-prompt-power-words`.

- Day 1: self-assess on the Ladder. Where are you honestly? Document.
- Day 2 to 5: use one power-word per day on a real piece of work. ultrathink on Monday, maxeffort on Tuesday, etc. Note which ones you reach for naturally.

**Done when:** you have used at least two power-words on real work that you would have otherwise sent without them.

---

## Week 4, brand voice

**Goal:** install `skills/01-brand-voice`.

- Day 1: fill in the brand-voice template with your company specifics. Voice attributes, banned words, signature phrases.
- Day 2 to 5: use it on at least one real message per day. Track how much you rewrite.

**Done when:** at least one draft you sent this week was the brand-voice draft, untouched.

---

## Week 5, board panel

**Goal:** install `skills/03-board-panel`.

- Day 1: customise the five lenses to fit your role and stakeholders.
- Day 2 to 5: run it before at least three real decisions.

**Done when:** the panel changed your mind on at least one decision.

---

## Week 6, commentary engine

**Goal:** install `skills/06-commentary-engine`.

- Use it on the next numerical readout you have to produce: variance commentary, KPI summary, OKR review, sales pipeline note.
- Compare its draft to what you would have written yourself. The five-move structure should make the difference visible.

**Done when:** you sent commentary that started as a commentary-engine draft.

---

## Week 7, morning brief, manual mode

**Goal:** install `skills/07-morning-brief`.

- Day 1: get your calendar in a form you can hand to Claude (export, screenshot, or connected calendar).
- Day 2 to 5: run it manually each morning. Compare to what you would have noticed yourself. The gaps are where it earns its keep.

**Done when:** the brief has surfaced at least one thing you would have otherwise missed.

---

## Week 8, audit and consolidate

**Goal:** look at every skill installed so far. Decide what to keep, refine, or kill.

- Reread your last five outputs from each skill. Mark each: useful / neutral / not useful.
- Anything with more than 40% not useful, rework the prompt or kill it.
- Anything with more than 60% useful, candidate for promotion to a scheduled agent.

**Done when:** you have made the cut. Quality stack over quantity stack.

---

## Week 9, first scheduled agent

**Goal:** promote `morning-brief` to a scheduled task.

- Day 1: set up the scheduled task in Claude Cowork (or ChatGPT Tasks, or n8n).
- Day 2 to 5: receive briefs Tuesday to Friday morning at 06:30 local time.
- Friday: review the week's briefs. Tune the prompt if signal-to-noise needs work.

**Done when:** you have replaced "checking the calendar in the morning" with "reading the brief."

---

## Week 10, second scheduled agent

**Goal:** schedule the market or industry digest you designed in week 2.

- Same install pattern as week 9.
- Friday: mark each item useful / neutral / not useful. Update your Profile with what to ignore.

**Done when:** you have stopped opening industry newsletters.

---

## Week 11, sensitivity templates

**Goal:** install `skills/10-sensitivity-templates`.

- Day 1: walk through the five patterns. Pick the one closest to a real situation you faced this quarter.
- Day 2 to 5: use it to draft a real deliverable that you would otherwise have built around the data wall.

**Done when:** you have shipped something that would have been blocked or sloppy without this skill.

---

## Week 12, sparring partner

**Goal:** install `skills/13-sparring-partner` and set your first quarterly theme.

- Day 1: pick the theme. Eight to twelve sub-elements.
- Day 2: schedule the weekly sparring session for Friday afternoons.
- Day 5: first sparring session. Note what shifted.

**Done when:** you have completed your first sparring session and the next one is on the calendar.

---

## At the end of 90 days

- Eight to fourteen hours per week reclaimed.
- A Profile that is twice as sharp as the one you wrote in week 1.
- Eight to ten skills installed and trusted.
- Two scheduled agents running reliably.
- A peer-shareable system. Yours. Portable. No vendor lock-in.

Time to repeat the loop. Refresh the Profile, set the next quarterly theme on the sparring partner, and either deepen what you have or add the skills you skipped in week 8.

# Quickstart

Twenty minutes from cloning this repo to your first useful skill output.

## Time required

- 3 minutes: install (see `install.md`, Path A or B)
- 5 minutes: memory bootstrap
- 10 minutes: setup interview, short version
- 2 minutes: use your first skill on real work

## Step 1, install (3 min)

Pick Path A from `install.md` if you do not want to use a terminal. Pick Path B if you do.

## Step 2, memory bootstrap (5 min)

Open `prompts/08-memory-bootstrap.txt`. Copy the whole thing into a new Claude conversation. Fill in the bracketed placeholders.

You do not need to fill in every line. You need to fill in honestly the parts that apply to you. Your job title, your company, your tools, the things specific to your environment that always matter. Two or three minutes of typing is enough to start.

Claude will confirm what it saved. From this point on, every conversation in this project is informed by what is now in memory.

## Step 3, setup interview (10 min)

Open `prompts/09-setup-interview.txt`. Copy into the same Claude conversation. Five rounds of questions. Answer briefly. After each round Claude summarizes what it learned, you correct it if needed, you move on.

At the end Claude proposes: which skills to install first, which connectors, which scheduled tasks, which stakeholders to loop in, your first-month roadmap.

This is your personal roadmap. Save it.

## Step 4, your first skill (2 min)

Pick the skill at the top of your roadmap. The most common first picks are:

- **brand-voice** if your job involves a lot of writing.
- **commentary-engine** if your job involves explaining numbers to anyone.
- **board-panel** if you are about to make a non-trivial decision.
- **morning-brief** if you would benefit from a daily briefing before 09:00.
- **superintelligence-ladder** if you want to assess where you are first.

Use the skill on one piece of real work you have to do today. Not a test case. Real work.

## Step 5, schedule (next week)

After you have used a few skills manually for a week and you trust them, schedule one. Most people start with `morning-brief` running at 07:00. See `skills/07-morning-brief/SKILL.md` for the schedule configuration.

## What you should feel by minute 25

- The output references something specific to your role or your company that you mentioned in memory bootstrap.
- The output gives you a clear next step, not a vague summary.
- You catch yourself thinking "I want to use this again tomorrow."

If none of those things happen, the memory bootstrap was too thin. Open it again, add three to five more lines about your work, save again. Try the skill again.

## What you should not feel

- Pressure to install every skill in week one. Pick two. Use them. Add the next one when the first two are reflex.
- A need to schedule the morning brief on day one. Manual first.
- An expectation that one prompt will replace your job. The skills replace small parts of your week. The compounding effect shows up around week three.

## Next thirty days

By day 30 you should have:

- Memory bootstrap done and refined once.
- Three skills installed and used on real work at least five times each.
- One scheduled task running reliably.
- Your `setup-interview` roadmap updated based on what you learned.

That is Level 4 on the ladder. Most people stay at Level 2.
